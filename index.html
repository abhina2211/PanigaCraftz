      Made by hand, at home in Sydney

      PanigaCraftz is Neha Koolwal's craft studio. A former lecturer, she started the business six years ago and loves giving colour to plain products. Every piece is decorated by hand using decoupage: paper designs are cut out, layered onto glass, wood, metal or ceramic and sealed with varnish, so no two pieces are exactly the same.
